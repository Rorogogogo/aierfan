# Privacy Policy — aierfan (Aiyifan Ads-Free)

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

## English

aierfan ("the extension") does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any
personal or sensitive user data.

- **No data collection.** The extension makes no network requests of its own and
  sends nothing to the developer or any third party.
- **Local settings only.** It uses Chrome's `storage` permission solely to save,
  on your own device, your on/off preferences (ad blocking, quality selection)
  and a local count of ads blocked. This never leaves your browser.
- **Scope.** It runs only on `kubb.tv` / `yfsp.tv` pages, where it suppresses
  scheduled mid-roll ads and selects the highest video quality already delivered
  to the player. It does not modify membership, purchases, account balances, or
  media URLs, and it does not access any other site.

## 简体中文

aierfan（“本扩展”）不收集、不存储、不传输、不出售任何个人或敏感用户数据。

- **不收集数据。** 本扩展自身不发起任何网络请求，不向开发者或任何第三方发送信息。
- **仅本地设置。** 使用 Chrome 的 `storage` 权限，仅用于在你自己的设备上保存你的开关
  偏好（广告拦截、画质选择）以及本地的拦截次数统计。这些数据不会离开你的浏览器。
- **作用范围。** 仅在 `kubb.tv` / `yfsp.tv` 页面运行，用于拦截定时中插广告，并在播放器
  已获取的视频流中选择最高画质。不修改会员、购买、账户余额或媒体地址，也不访问任何
  其他网站。
